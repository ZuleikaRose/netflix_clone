From Idea to blueprint: the planning pipeline

1. What is it?

   "A video streaming web application that allows users to watch movies and TV shows online  similar to Netflix. It will have features like user authentication content recommendations and a responsive design for different devices."
2. What Problem? What is the user problems? How do people solve it now? (spreadsheets, notebooks, whiteboards, etc)? The problem is users want to watch movies and TV shows online but they have to pay for multiple subscriptions or use illegal streaming websites. People currently solve this by subscribing to multiple streaming services or using illegal streaming websites which can be risky and unreliable.
3. Who are the users? Who is the target audience? The target audience is anyone who wants to watch movies and TV shows online, especially those who want a single platform that offers a wide variety of content without having to pay for multiple subscriptions.
4. Features: Reasons to log in? Create Read Update Delete Search Filter Sort Share? The service will be a subscription-based streaming platform that offers a wide variety of movies and TV shows for users to watch online. Access to a wide variety of movies and TV shows in one place. Personalized recommendations based on viewing history. Ability to create watch lists and save favorite content for later viewing. User authentication and account management. Responsive design for different devices (desktop, tablet, mobile). Search and filter options to find specific content. Social sharing options to share favorite content with friends and family.
5. User journey: The user journey starts with the user visiting the website and signing up for the first time. After signing up, the user can browse through the available movies and TV shows, search for specific content using the search bar, and filter content based on genre, release date, or popularity. The user can click on a movie or TV show to view its details, including a synopsis, cast information, and user reviews. They can add content to their watch list or mark it as a favorite for later viewing. When a user decides to watch a movie or TV show, they can click on the play button to start streaming. The user can also rate and review content after watching it. The website will provide personalized recommendations based on the user's viewing history and preferences. Users can manage their account settings, including updating their profile information and changing their password. The user journey ends with the user logging out of the website after they have finished watching content or managing their account. The website will provide customer support options for users who encounter issues or have questions about the platform. Overall, the user journey is designed to be intuitive and user-friendly, ensuring that users can easily navigate the website and enjoy a seamless streaming experience.
6. Screens: The screens will have a clean and modern design, with a focus on showcasing the available content. The homepage will feature a carousel of featured movies and TV shows, along with sections for popular content, new releases, and personalized recommendations. Each movie, or TV show will have its own dedicated page with detailed information, including a synopsis, cast and crew details, user reviews, and related content. The search and filter options will be easily accessible from the homepage and will allow users to quickly find specific content. The watch list and favorites sections will be prominently displayed, allowing users to easily access their saved content. The user account settings will be accessible from the homepage, allowing users to manage their profile information and preferences. The website will be designed to be responsive, ensuring that it looks and functions well on different devices, including desktops, tablets, and mobile phones. The overall design will prioritize user experience, making it easy for users to navigate the website and find the content they want to watch.
7. When Things Break? The website will have a dedicated support page where users can report issues or seek help. If the streaming service experiences downtime or technical issues, users will be notified through a banner on the homepage and via email if they are logged in. The website will also have a FAQ section to address common questions and concerns. In the event of a bug or error, users can submit a support ticket, and the development team will work to resolve the issue as quickly as possible. Regular maintenance and updates will be performed to ensure the website remains stable and secure. Users will be encouraged to provide feedback on their app experience, and this feedback will be used to improve the platform.
