From Idea to blueprint: the planning pipeline

1. What is it?

   "A video streaming web application that allows users to watch movies and TV shows online  similar to Netflix. It will have features like user authentication content recommendations and a responsive design for different devices."
2. What Problem? What is the user problems? How do people solve it now? (spreadsheets, notebooks, whiteboards, etc)? The problem is users want to watch movies and TV shows online but they have to pay for multiple subscriptions or use illegal streaming websites. People currently solve this by subscribing to multiple streaming services or using illegal streaming websites which can be risky and unreliable.
3. Who are the users? Who is the target audience? The target audience is anyone who wants to watch movies and TV shows online, especially those who want a single platform that offers a wide variety of content without having to pay for multiple subscriptions.
4. Features: Reasons to log in? Create Read Update Delete Search Filter Sort Share? The service will be a subscription-based streaming platform that offers a wide variety of movies and TV shows for users to watch online. Access to a wide variety of movies and TV shows in one place, including real, up-to-date movie and TV show information (posters, overviews, ratings, cast and crew) from The Movie Database (TMDB). Personalized recommendations based on viewing history, including recommendations from TMDB for the TMDB titles a member saves. Continue Watching that remembers where a member stopped in each title, on any device. Ability to create watch lists and save favorite content for later viewing, including movies and TV shows found on TMDB. User authentication and account management, with strong passwords and a forgot-password reset by email; a member's lists, ratings, reviews and settings are saved to their account and come back whenever they sign in, and members stay signed in when they refresh the page or come back later, until they sign out. Visitors can try everything with a private demo account. Responsive design for different devices (desktop, tablet, mobile). Search and filter options to find specific content. Social sharing options to share favorite content with friends and family. Accessibility features for everyone: an accessibility menu with one-tap profiles (seizure safe, low vision, ADHD friendly, dyslexia friendly, keyboard navigation, deaf and hard of hearing) and settings for text size, readable and dyslexia-friendly fonts, spacing, contrast, color, highlighted links and headings, a strong focus outline, a big cursor, a reading guide, hiding images and stopping animations. Full video player controls with closed captions, and control over autoplaying video. Quick games with optional leaderboards that show a member's first name and best score only if they turn them on. Up to five profiles per account (including Kids profiles), each with its own lists, maturity rating, Continue Watching, history and scores. Actor and director pages. Seasons and episodes for every show. Reminders for upcoming TMDB releases with a notifications bell. Viewing history with privacy controls (pause it, delete entries, delete it automatically). Custom lists that can be shared by link. Watch parties that keep friends' playback in step, with chat. Search that forgives typos, suggests titles, people and genres, and remembers recent searches. A support dashboard for the support team. An installable app that opens offline. A PIN lock for any profile, and Kids profiles that need a grown-up's account password before account settings open. Two-step sign-in with an authenticator app, and a list of the devices signed in to the account with a way to sign them out. Skip Intro and Skip Recap buttons, chapter marks on the progress bar, picture previews while seeking and picture-in-picture in the video player. A Year in Review page built from each profile's viewing history. A "What should I watch?" assistant that suggests titles from a request in plain words, using AI. Quick thumbs ratings (Not for me, I like this, Love this!) that steer recommendations, with Not for me hiding a title from them. One-time donations by card through Stripe, from a round coffee button on every page, with a list of the member's past donations and an emailed receipt. A Surprise me button that opens a random title, one of the platform's own or a movie or TV show from TMDB. Keyboard shortcuts help on any page. Daily screen-time limits for profiles. Captions in English or Spanish. Downloads to watch offline. Reporting a review to the support team. Helpful votes on reviews, with reviews sorted by most helpful or newest. A weekly screen-time summary for parents. Audio description in the video player. Monthly donations that can be cancelled any time. A donations view on the support dashboard. The whole interface in English or Spanish.
5. User journey: The user journey starts with the user visiting the website and signing up for the first time. After signing up, the user can browse through the available movies and TV shows, search for specific content using the search bar, and filter content based on genre, release date, or popularity. Alongside the platform's own titles, the user can browse trending and popular movies and TV shows from The Movie Database (TMDB), find TMDB titles in search results and open any of them to read its overview, rating, cast and crew, watch its trailer, see where it streams, rents or sells in the U.S., rate and review it, and find more like it. TMDB titles carry their real U.S. maturity ratings, so restricted profiles see the ones that fit. The user can click on a movie or TV show to view its details, including a synopsis, cast information, and user reviews. They can add content to their watch list or mark it as a favorite for later viewing. When a user decides to watch a movie or TV show, they can click on the play button to start streaming, then use the player controls to pause, seek, skip 10 seconds, mute or set the volume (which follows them to any device), change speed, go to the previous or next episode of a TV show, go full screen and turn closed captions on or off. When they stop, the player remembers where, so Continue Watching on the homepage (on any device they sign in on) takes them back to that point, or to the next episode once they finish one. Previews only play by themselves when the user turns on autoplay, and autoplay is always off when they ask for reduced motion. At any point the user can open the accessibility menu from the top of the page to adjust text, color, navigation aids and media settings. The user can also rate and review content after watching it. The website will provide personalized recommendations based on the user's viewing history and preferences. Users can manage their account settings, including updating their profile information and changing their password. If they forget their password, they choose "Forgot your password?" on the login page, receive a reset link by email and set a new password. A visitor who doesn't want to sign up yet can choose "Use demo account" to get a private demo account that is deleted after 24 hours. If they want to leave, they can delete their account from a danger zone in account settings after cancelling their subscription; this removes their access to the app, and they can contact support to have their data removed. The user journey ends with the user logging out of the website after they have finished watching content or managing their account. The website will provide customer support options for users who encounter issues or have questions about the platform. Overall, the user journey is designed to be intuitive and user-friendly, ensuring that users can easily navigate the website and enjoy a seamless streaming experience. After signing in the user picks who's watching (or adds a profile, such as a Kids profile), and each profile keeps its own lists and history. From a title the user can open an actor's or director's page, browse a show's seasons and play any episode, add the title to their own named lists and share a list by link, or start a watch party and send friends the invite link so everyone watches in step and chats. In New & Hot the user sets reminders for films that aren't out yet, and the bell in the header announces them on release day, along with replies from support. The user can review and delete their viewing history, pause it, and install the app on their phone or computer. A parent can lock their own profile with a four-digit PIN (after confirming the account password), so choosing it on Who's watching asks for the PIN; a child on a Kids profile who opens Account or Manage profiles is asked for the account password first. In Account → Security the user turns on two-step sign-in by scanning a code with an authenticator app and entering the six-digit code it shows; from then on, signing in asks for a code after the password. The same section lists the devices signed in to the account, and the user can sign out any of them, or all of them except this one. While watching, the user can skip a show's recap and intro, see the chapter they're in, preview a frame by pointing at the progress bar, and keep the video playing in a small window while they browse (picture-in-picture). Once a year (or any time) the user opens Year in Review to see how many titles, episodes and hours they watched, their favourite genres and actors, their longest streak and busiest month, and shares a summary with friends. When the user doesn't know what to watch, they ask the assistant in their own words ("something tense under two hours") and get a few titles with a reason for each, always within their profile's maturity rating. On any title the user can tap Not for me, I like this or Love this!; loved and liked titles shape Because You Liked, and titles marked Not for me stop appearing in recommendations. A member who enjoys the app taps the round coffee button at the bottom right of any page, picks $5, $10, $25, $50 or another amount (from $1 to $10,000), enters their card and taps Donate; they see a thank-you with the amount, or a plain explanation if the card is declined; if their bank is still processing the payment they're told so, and trying again after a declined card or a dropped connection never charges twice. Below the form, Your donations lists each past donation with its amount, date and whether it went through, and Stripe emails a receipt. When nothing comes to mind, the user taps Surprise me on Home or Search and a random title they're allowed to watch opens: one of the platform's own, or a movie or TV show from TMDB. Pressing ? on any page shows every keyboard shortcut. A parent sets a daily limit on a Kids profile (with the account password); once the child has watched that long, the player stops with Time's up for today, and only the account password allows 30 more minutes. A viewer who reads Spanish chooses Caption language → Español in the accessibility menu. Before a trip the user downloads a few titles from their pages and watches them later with no connection from My App → Downloads. If another member's review gives away the ending or is offensive, the user taps Report under it and picks a reason; the support team sees it on the support dashboard and keeps or removes the review. Under a review they found useful, the user taps Helpful, and Sort reviews puts the most helpful first. In Profiles → Edit → Screen time a parent sees how long a limited profile watched on each of the last 7 days. A viewer who can't see the screen turns on Audio description (the AD button, the D key or the accessibility menu) and the player reads out what happens, pausing while it speaks. On the donation page a member can choose Monthly; Your monthly donation (also in Account) shows the amount and the next payment and cancels it. Support staff open Donations on the support dashboard to see totals by month and open any payment in Stripe. A Spanish speaker chooses Language → Español in the accessibility menu (or arrives in Spanish when their browser is), and the whole app switches.
6. Screens: The screens will have a clean and modern design, with a focus on showcasing the available content. The homepage will feature a carousel of featured movies and TV shows, along with sections for popular content, new releases, and personalized recommendations. Each movie, or TV show will have its own dedicated page with detailed information, including a synopsis, cast and crew details, user reviews, and related content. The search and filter options will be easily accessible from the homepage and will allow users to quickly find specific content. The watch list and favorites sections will be prominently displayed, allowing users to easily access their saved content. The user account settings will be accessible from the homepage, allowing users to manage their profile information and preferences. Every screen has an accessibility menu button in the header and an Accessibility Policy link in the footer. The website will be designed to be responsive, ensuring that it looks and functions well on different devices, including desktops, tablets, and mobile phones. The overall design will prioritize user experience, making it easy for users to navigate the website and find the content they want to watch.
7. When Things Break? The website will have a dedicated support page where users can report issues or seek help. If the streaming service experiences downtime or technical issues, users will be notified through a banner on the homepage and via email if they are logged in. The website will also have a FAQ section to address common questions and concerns. In the event of a bug or error, users can submit a support ticket, and the development team will work to resolve the issue as quickly as possible. Regular maintenance and updates will be performed to ensure the website remains stable and secure. Users will be encouraged to provide feedback on their app experience, and this feedback will be used to improve the platform.

The 8-Question Practical Template

1. What problem does the app solve? The app solves the problem of users having to pay for multiple streaming subscriptions or resorting to illegal streaming websites. It provides a single platform where users can access a wide variety of movies and TV shows legally and conveniently.
2. Who are the users? Who are the users? Who is the target audience? The target audience is anyone who wants to watch movies and TV shows online, especially those who want a single platform that offers a wide variety of content without having to pay for multiple subscriptions. The users are likely to be tech-savy individuals who enjoy consuming digital media and are looking for a convient and legal way to access their favorite movies and TV shows. They may include young adults, families, and individuals who are looking for a cost-effective alternative to multiple streaming subscriptions. The users may also be interested in personalized recommendations and the ability to create watch lists and save favorite content for later viewing. Overall, the target audience is diverse, but they share a common interest in accessing high-quality entertainment content online.
3. What are their primary goals? The primary goals of the users are to have access to a wide variety of movies and TV shows in one place, to be able to discover new content through personalized recommendations, and to have a convenient and legal way to watch their favorite entertainment content online. Users also want the ability to create watch lists, save favorite content for later viewing, and manage their account settings easily. Additionally, users may seek a seamless streaming service that works well across different devices, including desktops, tablets, and mobile phones. Users with disabilities want to adjust the app to how they read, see, hear and move, watch with captions, and stop video from playing by itself. Families want to keep children out of grown-up profiles and settings, members want their account protected beyond a password, and everyone wants to get to the story faster (skipping recaps and intros) and find something to watch without scrolling for ages. Overall, the primary goals of the users are to have a user-friendly and enjoyable streaming experience that meets their entertainment needs.
