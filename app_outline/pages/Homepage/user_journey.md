From Idea to blueprint: the planning pipeline

1. What is it?

   "A video streaming web application that allows users to watch movies and TV shows online  similar to Netflix. It will have features like user authentication content recommendations and a responsive design for different devices."
2. What Problem? What is the user problems? How do people solve it now? (spreadsheets, notebooks, whiteboards, etc)? The problem is users want to watch movies and TV shows online but they have to pay for multiple subscriptions or use illegal streaming websites. People currently solve this by subscribing to multiple streaming services or using illegal streaming websites which can be risky and unreliable.
3. Who are the users? Who is the target audience? The target audience is anyone who wants to watch movies and TV shows online, especially those who want a single platform that offers a wide variety of content without having to pay for multiple subscriptions.
4. Features: Reasons to log in? Create Read Update Delete Search Filter Sort Share? The service will be a subscription-based streaming platform that offers a wide variety of movies and TV shows for users to watch online. Access to a wide variety of movies and TV shows in one place. Personalized recommendations based on viewing history. Ability to create watch lists and save favorite content for later viewing. User authentication and account management, with strong passwords and a forgot-password reset by email; a member's lists, ratings, reviews and settings are saved to their account and come back whenever they sign in, and members stay signed in when they refresh the page or come back later, until they sign out. Visitors can try everything with a private demo account. Responsive design for different devices (desktop, tablet, mobile). Search and filter options to find specific content. Social sharing options to share favorite content with friends and family. Accessibility features for everyone: an accessibility menu with one-tap profiles (seizure safe, low vision, ADHD friendly, dyslexia friendly, keyboard navigation, deaf and hard of hearing) and settings for text size, readable and dyslexia-friendly fonts, spacing, contrast, color, highlighted links and headings, a strong focus outline, a big cursor, a reading guide, hiding images and stopping animations. Full video player controls with closed captions, and control over autoplaying video.
5. User journey: The user journey starts with the user visiting the website and signing up for the first time. After signing up, the user can browse through the available movies and TV shows, search for specific content using the search bar, and filter content based on genre, release date, or popularity. The user can click on a movie or TV show to view its details, including a synopsis, cast information, and user reviews. They can add content to their watch list or mark it as a favorite for later viewing. When a user decides to watch a movie or TV show, they can click on the play button to start streaming, then use the player controls to pause, seek, skip 10 seconds, mute, change speed, go full screen and turn closed captions on or off. Previews only play by themselves when the user turns on autoplay, and autoplay is always off when they ask for reduced motion. At any point the user can open the accessibility menu from the top of the page to adjust text, color, navigation aids and media settings. The user can also rate and review content after watching it. The website will provide personalized recommendations based on the user's viewing history and preferences. Users can manage their account settings, including updating their profile information and changing their password. If they forget their password, they choose "Forgot your password?" on the login page, receive a reset link by email and set a new password. A visitor who doesn't want to sign up yet can choose "Use demo account" to get a private demo account that is deleted after 24 hours. If they want to leave, they can delete their account from a danger zone in account settings after cancelling their subscription; this removes their access to the app, and they can contact support to have their data removed. The user journey ends with the user logging out of the website after they have finished watching content or managing their account. The website will provide customer support options for users who encounter issues or have questions about the platform. Overall, the user journey is designed to be intuitive and user-friendly, ensuring that users can easily navigate the website and enjoy a seamless streaming experience.
6. Screens: The screens will have a clean and modern design, with a focus on showcasing the available content. The homepage will feature a carousel of featured movies and TV shows, along with sections for popular content, new releases, and personalized recommendations. Each movie, or TV show will have its own dedicated page with detailed information, including a synopsis, cast and crew details, user reviews, and related content. The search and filter options will be easily accessible from the homepage and will allow users to quickly find specific content. The watch list and favorites sections will be prominently displayed, allowing users to easily access their saved content. The user account settings will be accessible from the homepage, allowing users to manage their profile information and preferences. Every screen has an accessibility menu button in the header and an Accessibility Policy link in the footer. The website will be designed to be responsive, ensuring that it looks and functions well on different devices, including desktops, tablets, and mobile phones. The overall design will prioritize user experience, making it easy for users to navigate the website and find the content they want to watch.
7. When Things Break? The website will have a dedicated support page where users can report issues or seek help. If the streaming service experiences downtime or technical issues, users will be notified through a banner on the homepage and via email if they are logged in. The website will also have a FAQ section to address common questions and concerns. In the event of a bug or error, users can submit a support ticket, and the development team will work to resolve the issue as quickly as possible. Regular maintenance and updates will be performed to ensure the website remains stable and secure. Users will be encouraged to provide feedback on their app experience, and this feedback will be used to improve the platform.

The 8-Question Practical Template

1. What problem does the app solve? The app solves the problem of users having to pay for multiple streaming subscriptions or resorting to illegal streaming websites. It provides a single platform where users can access a wide variety of movies and TV shows legally and conveniently.
2. Who are the users? Who are the users? Who is the target audience? The target audience is anyone who wants to watch movies and TV shows online, especially those who want a single platform that offers a wide variety of content without having to pay for multiple subscriptions. The users are likely to be tech-savy individuals who enjoy consuming digital media and are looking for a convient and legal way to access their favorite movies and TV shows. They may include young adults, families, and individuals who are looking for a cost-effective alternative to multiple streaming subscriptions. The users may also be interested in personalized recommendations and the ability to create watch lists and save favorite content for later viewing. Overall, the target audience is diverse, but they share a common interest in accessing high-quality entertainment content online.
3. What are their primary goals? The primary goals of the users are to have access to a wide variety of movies and TV shows in one place, to be able to discover new content through personalized recommendations, and to have a convenient and legal way to watch their favorite entertainment content online. Users also want the ability to create watch lists, save favorite content for later viewing, and manage their account settings easily. Additionally, users may seek a seamless streaming service that works well across different devices, including desktops, tablets, and mobile phones. Users with disabilities want to adjust the app to how they read, see, hear and move, watch with captions, and stop video from playing by itself. Overall, the primary goals of the users are to have a user-friendly and enjoyable streaming experience that meets their entertainment needs.
4. What information do the users need? The users need information about the available movies and TV shows, including titles, genres, release dates, synopses, cast and crew details, user reviews, and ratings. They also need information about their account settings, subscription plans, payment options, and any promotions or discounts available. Additionally, users may need information about the website's features, such as how to create watch lists, save favorite content, and access personalized recommendations. Users also need an accessibility policy explaining the accessibility standard the platform follows, its accessibility features, video player keyboard shortcuts, known limitations and how to get help. Users may also need information about technical requirements for streaming content, such as supported devices and internet speed recommendations. Overall, the users need comprehensive and easily accessible information to make informed decisions about their streaming experience and account management.
5. What actions can the users perform? The users can perform actions such as signing up for an account, logging in and out, browsing available movies and TV shows, searching for specific content, filtering content by genre or release date, viewing detailed information about movies and TV shows, adding content to their watch list or marking it as a favorite, streaming content, rating and reviewing content, managing their account settings, and accessing customer support options. Users can also share their favorite content with friends and family through social sharing options. Users can customize accessibility settings, control video playback (including closed captions and playback speed), turn autoplaying previews and next episodes on or off, and delete their account access once their subscription is cancelled. Additionally, users can provide feedback on their app experience, report issues or bugs, and provide suggestions for improvements. Overall, the users have a range of actions they can perform to enhance their streaming experience and manage their account online.
6. What pages are required for the Nexflix App Users? The required pages for the Netflix App users include:
   -Homepage: Featuring a carousel of featured movies and TV shows, popular content, new releases, and personalized recommendations.
   -Movie/TV Show Detail Page: Providing detailed information about each movie or TV show, including synopsis, cast and crew details, user reviews, and related content, plus a video player that plays a short open-licensed sample clip (from the Creative Commons Blender films, credited on screen) with real caption files, controls (play/pause, seek, skip 10 seconds, mute, playback speed, full screen) and a closed captions toggle.
   -Search Results Page: Displaying search results based on user guided query and allowing users to filter results by genre, release date, or popularity.
   -Watch list Page: Allowing users to view and manage their saved content for later viewing.
   -Favorites Page: Allowing users to view and manage their favorite content.
   -Account Settings Page: Allowing users to manage their profile information, subscription plans, billing plans (such  as change billing date, cancel, subscription and pause subscription) payment options, and preferences, including autoplay previews and autoplay next episode (which actually start or stop video), an accessibility section, and a red Delete Account button in a danger zone. Deleting asks the user to: cancel their subscription first (delete won't work until it is cancelled, and it only removes their access to the app); type their email address and enter their password to confirm; and contact support to submit a ticket if they also want their data removed.
   -Customer Support Page: Providing users with options to report issues, seek help, and access FAQs.
   -Login/Signup Page: Allowing users to create an account or log in to their existing account, or try a private demo account. New passwords need at least 13 characters with a lowercase letter, an uppercase letter, a digit and a symbol, and passwords known from data leaks are refused.
   -Forgot Password / Reset Password Pages: Allowing users who forgot their password to request a reset link by email, then choose a new password from that link (expired links ask for a new one).
   -Logout Page: Allowing users to log out of their account securely.
   -Legal and Privacy Pages: Providing users with information about the website's terms of service, privacy policy, and any legal disclaimers related to content streaming.
   -Error/Issue Reporting Page: Allowing users to report bugs, errors, or technical issues they encounter while using the platform.
   -Feedback Page: Allowing users to provide feedback on their app experience and suggest improvements for the platform.
   -Promotions/Discounts Page: Providing users with information about any ongoing promotions or discounts available for subscription plans or content access.
   -Technical Requirements Page: Providing users with information about supported devices, internet speed recommendations, and any other technical requirements for streaming content effectively.
   -FAQs Page: Addressing common questions and concerns related to the streaming service, account managmeent, and content process.
   -Terms of Service Page: Outlining the rules and guidlines for using the streaming platform, including user responsiblities and limitations of liability.-Privacy Policy Page: Detailing how managed user data is collected, stored, and used by the streaming service including information about cookies, data sharing, and user content.
   -AI Integration Page: Providing users with information about any AI-powered features or recommendations available on the platform, including how AI is used to enhance the user experience and improve content discovery.
   -Accessibility Policy Page: Linked in the footer of every page. Explains the platform's commitment and the WCAG 2.2 AA standard it follows, lists the accessibility features (the accessibility menu, closed captions, keyboard-accessible player controls, autoplay control), video player keyboard shortcuts, known limitations, and how to report an accessibility issue.
   -Accessibility Menu (on every page): A menu like the OneTap accessibility app, opened from an icon in the page header, with one-tap profiles and settings for text size, fonts, line height, letter spacing, text alignment, contrast, saturation, highlighted links and headings, strong focus outline, big cursor, reading guide, hiding images, stopping animations, closed captions and caption size, and autoplay.
   -Affiliate Program Page: Providing information about my affiliate program, including how users can participate, earn commissions from referrals, and access marketing materials to promote the streaming service. This page will outline the terms and conditions of the affiliate program, including eligibility requirements, commission structure, payment methods, and any restrictions or limitations on promotional activities. Users will also find resources and support for optimizing their affiliate marketing efforts, such as tips for driving traffic, tracking referrals, and maximizing earnings. The Affiliate Program Page will serve as a comprehensive guide for users interested in becoming affiliates and promoting the streaming platform to their audience, helping to expand the reach of the service and attract new subscribers through word-of-mouth marketing and online promotion.
7. What data must be collected? The data that must be collected includes user account information (such as name, email address, and password), subscription plan details, payment information (such as credit card or billing details), viewing history and preferences for personalized recommendations, user ratings and reviews for movies and TV shows, watch lists and favorite content, search queries and filter settings, device information (such as device type and operating system), technical requirements for streaming content (such as internet speed and supported devices), feedback and suggestions from users, bug reports and error logs, promotional and discount usage data, accessibility settings (text, font, color, navigation aids, captions and caption size), autoplay preferences, account deletion requests (when access was removed) and data removal tickets, the visitor's IP address for rate limiting public features such as demo accounts and referral clicks (kept for one day), and any other relevant data necessary for  the functionality and improvement of the streaming platform. Additionally, data related to user interactions with the website, such as click-through rates, time spent on pages, and engagement with content, may also be collected to enhance the user experience and optimize content recommendations. All data collection will be conducted in compliance with privacy regulations and best practices to ensure the security and confidentiality of user information.
8. What permissions exist? The permissions that exist include user consent for data collection and usage, access to user account information permission granted by the user, permission to send notifications and updates related to the streaming service, permission to access device information for technical requirements and compatibility, permission to collect and analyze viewing history and preferences for personalized recommendations, permission to access and manage watch lists and favorite content, permission to collect feedback and suggestions from users, permission to access user ratings and reviews for movies and TV shows, permission to access search queries and filter settings, permission to collect promotional and discount usage data. Permission to access and analyze user interactions with the website, such as (click-through rates, time spent on pages, and engagement with content for optimization purposes), and permission to comply with legal and regulatory requirements related to data privacy and security. Users will have the ability to manage their permissions and preferences through their account settings, including opting out of certain data collection practices and adjusting notification preferences. Users control whether video autoplays (previews and next episodes) and whether captions are shown. Users can delete their account access themselves once their subscription is cancelled, and can request removal of their data through a support ticket. Each member can only see and change their own data (row-level security); reviews always show the real author's name; promo codes are checked on the server; and only the platform's server functions can create demo accounts, remove account access or count referral clicks. The streaming platform will ensure that all permissions are clearly communicated to users and that their privacy is respected at all times.
